    Rotor · tree & building wakes
    Streaks are real air parcels carried by the modelled wind, coloured by what your wing would feel: net climb = updraught minus your sink at the speed you must fly to hold position. Trees, hedges and buildings come from 1 m LiDAR and satellite imagery and shed their own wakes. Thermals and sea breeze are not modelled. Educational only.
